- **Phase:** Definition
- **Status:** Active
- **Owner:** Sujit

## Decision
Clinic onboarding as in `flows/clinic-onboarding.md`:
1. Two paths on one form: Sales-assisted (BEST Key Account Manager) or Self-onboarding (clinic). Link shared by BEST; not public.
2. Clinic chooses its plan; license created from it after approval.
3. BEST Platform Admin approves, rejects with reason, or sends back for changes.
4. Signed BAA and service agreement mandatory before approval.
5. Wearables supplied by BEST and registered to the clinic after approval.
6. Clinic Active at Org Admin's first login.
7. New role: BEST Key Account Manager.
8. Defaults accepted: 14-day link, drafts, duplicate EIN/NPI block, unlimited resubmits, 7-day Org Admin invite, Org Admin uses a seat, mandatory clinic details, multiple locations, statuses.

## Rationale
Confirmed by Sujit 2026-10-14. Replaces the single "Onboard organization" step in D-015's license flow.
