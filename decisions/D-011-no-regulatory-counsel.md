# D-011: No regulatory counsel gate; wearable category decided internally

- **Date:** 2026-10-09
- **Phase:** Definition
- **Status:** Active
- **Owner:** Sujit
- **Supersedes:** D-001 (gating on counsel's opinion and fallbacks removed; live indicators and soft alerts stay in MVP)
- **Closes:** Q-02 (no counsel engagement)

## Decision
There will be no regulatory counsel review. The category of wearable used is decided internally by BEST and TBR. Live indicators and soft alerts stay in the MVP without a gate.

## Rationale
Sujit's call after team discussion.

## Risk accepted
Real-time interpretation of children's physiological signals may still fall under FDA oversight (Jan 2026 CDS guidance). Mitigation relies on the clinical language rules, rule-based soft alerts, and the internal choice of wearable category.

## Revisit Trigger
BEST raises regulatory concerns, or the product's claims change.
