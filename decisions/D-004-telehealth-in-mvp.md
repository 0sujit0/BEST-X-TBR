- **Status:** Active — pending BEST confirmation
- **Owner:** Sujit
- **Supersedes:** —

## Decision
Revised PRD keeps §20 telehealth in MVP and adds it to §11 phasing. Risks are listed explicitly.

## Rationale
Consistent with D-001: preserve BEST's scope and let BEST decide with the risks visible.

## Options Rejected
| Option | Why rejected |
|---|---|
| Telehealth without wearables | Diverges from BEST's stated scope |
| Phase 2 | Same |

## Risks to state
- Video vendor must be HIPAA-eligible and sign a BAA.
- Someone at home must put the wearable on the child, but parents are non-users (§4).
- Consent flow depends on the consent store (D-006).
- Adds significant scope to MVP.

## Revisit Trigger
BEST confirms remote session volume is low, or vendor/BAA constraints block it.
