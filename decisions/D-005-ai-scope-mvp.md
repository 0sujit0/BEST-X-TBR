# D-005: MVP AI limited to summary drafting

- **Date:** 2026-10-09
- **Phase:** Definition
- **Status:** Active — proposed; pending BEST confirmation
- **Owner:** Sujit
- **Supersedes:** —
- **Closes:** Q-09 (TBR side)

## Decision
MVP AI = drafting post-session summaries (reviewable, editable, labelled AI-assisted). §19 pattern prompts and cross-session comparison move to Phase 2, matching §11. Adaptive alert tuning moves to Future.

## Rationale
Patterns need ≥ N completed sessions per child, so there is little to show early in a pilot. Resolves the §11 vs §14.2 / §19 conflict. Lowers regulatory exposure while D-001 keeps live alerts.

## Options Rejected
| Option | Why rejected |
|---|---|
| Summaries + pattern prompts | Little data early; contradicts §11 |
| Full §14.2 | Adaptive alert tuning adds risk on top of D-001 |

## Revisit Trigger
Pilot has enough completed sessions per child to make patterns meaningful.
